## In one paragraph

A single car-wash shop gets a web and Android platform where clients register their vehicles and
book a wash with real availability, the administrator manages the catalog, schedule, bays,
bookings and payments, and operators follow their assigned work. Payments are made by QR or
transfer outside the platform and verified by the administrator from an uploaded receipt.
