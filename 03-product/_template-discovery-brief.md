# Discovery Brief — Vehicle Washing

## 1. Discovery Objective

Understand how customers, operators, and administrators currently manage vehicle-washing services and identify the main problems, needs, expectations, and opportunities that Vehicle Washing should address.

---

## 2. Research Questions

### Customers

- How do customers currently request vehicle-washing services?
- How do they schedule appointments?
- What difficulties do they experience?
- What information do they need before booking?
- What would make the booking process easier?
- How do they currently track their services and payments?

### Operators

- How are services currently assigned?
- How do operators know which vehicles they must attend?
- How are service statuses communicated?
- What information is required to execute a service?
- What problems occur during service execution?

### Administrators

- How are users managed?
- How are services and prices managed?
- How are promotions managed?
- How are appointments monitored?
- How are payments controlled?
- Which processes are currently manual?

## 6. Assumptions to Validate

### A1

Customers need a faster and more organized way to schedule vehicle-washing services.

### A2

Customers value having vehicle and appointment information centralized.

### A3

Operators need a clear view of assigned services and their status.

### A4

Administrators need centralized management of users, services, promotions, and payments.

### A5

Digital appointment management can reduce manual coordination.

---

## 7. Expected Findings

The following are hypotheses, not confirmed findings:

- Manual booking may create scheduling friction.
- Customers may have difficulty tracking appointment information.
- Operators may benefit from centralized operational information.
- Administrators may benefit from better visibility of commercial activity.

These hypotheses must be validated through discovery activities.

## 9. Product Decisions

Initial product decisions based on the current problem framing:

- Provide digital appointment management.
- Centralize customer vehicle information.
- Separate operational and commercial responsibilities.
- Provide role-based access.
- Support promotion and payment management.
- Provide customers with service information.

These decisions should be reviewed as discovery evidence becomes available.
