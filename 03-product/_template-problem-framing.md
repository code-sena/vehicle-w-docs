# Problem Framing — Vehicle Washing

## 1. Problem

Managing vehicle-washing services can involve manual communication, fragmented information, and disconnected processes for requesting, scheduling, providing, and tracking services.

---

## 2. Who Has the Problem?

### Customers

Vehicle owners who need to schedule and manage washing services.

### Operators

Employees responsible for executing vehicle-washing services.

### Administrators

People responsible for managing users, services, appointments, promotions, payments, and business operations.

---

## 3. User Problem

Customers may have difficulty:

- Finding service information.
- Knowing available schedules.
- Scheduling appointments.
- Tracking appointment status.
- Managing vehicle information.
- Reviewing service history.
- Managing payment information.

Operators may have difficulty:

- Knowing assigned services.
- Accessing vehicle information.
- Tracking service status.
- Coordinating service execution.

Administrators may have difficulty:

- Managing users.
- Managing services.
- Controlling appointments.
- Managing promotions.
- Monitoring payments.

---

## 4. When Does the Problem Occur?

The problem may occur during:

1. Service discovery.
2. Appointment scheduling.
3. Appointment changes or cancellation.
4. Service assignment.
5. Service execution.
6. Payment.
7. Service history consultation.

---

## 5. Impact

Potential impacts include:

- Longer booking processes.
- Scheduling errors.
- Duplicate appointments.
- Information fragmentation.
- Limited visibility.
- Increased manual work.
- Poor customer experience.
- Difficulty monitoring operations.

These impacts should be validated through discovery.

---

## 6. Current Workaround

Potential current alternatives include:

- Phone calls.
- Messaging applications.
- In-person requests.
- Manual records.
- Spreadsheets.
- Informal communication between employees.

These alternatives must be validated with actual users.

---

## 7. Desired Future State

Customers should be able to:

```text
Register
   ↓
Manage Vehicle
   ↓
Browse Service
   ↓
Check Availability
   ↓
Book Appointment
   ↓
Apply Promotion
   ↓
Manage Payment
   ↓
Track Service
   ↓
Review History
```

Operators should have clear visibility of assigned services and their execution status.

Administrators should have centralized control of users and business processes relevant to their responsibilities.

---

## 8. Product Opportunity

### Security

Provide secure identity, authentication, and authorization.

### Operational

Provide reliable vehicle and service-execution management.

### Commercial

Provide service catalog, appointment, promotion, and payment management.

### Frontends

Provide an appropriate experience for customers, operators, and administrators.

---

## 9. Assumptions

The current problem framing assumes that:

- Customers need centralized service information.
- Customers value digital scheduling.
- Operators need better operational visibility.
- Administrators need centralized management.
- Digital processes can reduce manual coordination.

These assumptions require validation.

---

## 10. Out of Scope

The problem framing does not include:

- Mechanical repair.
- Vehicle sales.
- Fuel.
- Insurance.
- Advanced fleet management.
- Real-time GPS.
- AI recommendations.
- Multi-business marketplace.
- International expansion.
- Advanced BI.

---

## 11. Problem Statement

Vehicle owners and vehicle-washing businesses need a more organized way to manage the complete vehicle-washing process because manual communication and fragmented information can make scheduling, coordination, execution, and tracking more difficult.

Vehicle Washing addresses this opportunity through a centralized digital experience supported by independent Security, Operational, and Commercial services.

---

## 12. Problem Validation Goal

The goal of discovery is to determine:

- Whether the problem is real.
- How frequently it occurs.
- Who experiences it most strongly.
- What current alternatives users employ.
- Which problems should be prioritized.
- Which assumptions should be rejected or modified.
